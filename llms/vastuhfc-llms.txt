# Vastu Housing Finance Corporation

> Vastu Housing Finance Corporation Limited (Vastu HFC) is an Indian, technology-enabled affordable housing finance company. It provides home purchase, construction, extension, and improvement loans, plot-plus-construction loans, commercial property loans, and loans against property across 200+ branches in 17 states, and participates in government subsidy schemes such as Pradhan Mantri Awas Yojana Urban 2.0. Vehicle and business lending is offered through its subsidiary Vastu Finserve.

Vastu HFC does not publish a public developer portal, API documentation, or
machine-readable API specifications. This llms.txt was generated by the
API Evangelist enrichment pipeline from the company's public website.

## Products

- [Home Loan](https://www.vastuhfc.com/home-loan): Loans for purchasing residential property
- [Home Construction Loan](https://www.vastuhfc.com/home-construction-loan): Financing for self-construction of homes
- [Home Extension Loan](https://www.vastuhfc.com/home-extension-loan): Loans for extending or improving an existing home
- [Plot + Construction Loan](https://www.vastuhfc.com/plot-construction-loan): Combined plot purchase and construction financing
- [Commercial Property Loan](https://www.vastuhfc.com/commercial-property-loan): Loans for commercial property purchase
- [Loan Against Property](https://www.vastuhfc.com/loan-against-property): Secured lending against owned property
- [Green Homes](https://www.vastuhfc.com/Green-homes): Financing for environmentally sustainable homes
- [Pradhan Mantri Awas Yojana Urban 2.0](https://www.vastuhfc.com/Pradhan-Mantri-Awas-Yojana-Urban-2-0-Interest-Subsidy-Scheme): Government interest-subsidy scheme participation
